# Phone v4 provenance

The master was produced with built-in Imagegen as an edit of the existing v2 paper-phone asset. The user supplied a YouTube reference for the placement of screen damage.

The generated image shows a powered-off black, modern Pro Max-style phone with cracks concentrated at the bottom-left and a narrow torn-paper edge. It is an artistic phone depiction, with no claim to reproduce an exact product model.

`scripts/prepare-phone-v4.py` creates the published WebP and responsive variants. The original PNG is retained locally at `qa/phone-v4/master.png` (ignored by Git); the full-size export preserves its original 1254 × 1254 canvas and all RGBA pixels without cropping, compositing, recoloring, sharpening, or other creative changes. Responsive files use proportional Lanczos resizing and lossless WebP encoding, including transparency. `manifest.json` records dimensions, alpha bounds, file sizes, and the original master's SHA-256.

## Generation prompt

Built-in Imagegen; `transparent_background: true`. Inputs, in order: the existing `site/hero/desk/v2/phone.webp` and the user's `codex-clipboard-21e074df-dfb1-4d10-9695-4d47908aed77.png`.

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Project asset: a single modern black Pro Max-style smartphone, as a real photograph printed on matte paper and carefully torn out, for a collage portfolio. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET and paper-material/straight-front composition reference. Image 2 is ONLY the damage-placement reference: realistic shattered glass mainly at the bottom. Replace the phone in image 1 with a contemporary large black iPhone Pro Max-inspired front, flat black titanium edges, slim even bezels, softly rounded corners, subtle black pill-shaped camera island near the top. Do not claim an exact unreleased model. The display is POWERED OFF: almost pure black, NO clock, NO text, no app, no logo, no wallpaper, no colored light. Damage: one believable impact cluster at the bottom-left corner and fine white hairline spiderweb cracks across the lowest 20–25 percent, just 1–2 finer cracks reaching into the lower middle. Upper 65 percent of the glass is clean dark black. The cracks must remain sharp fine photographed glass, never thick white drawings. Frame undamaged enough to read as a modern slim black phone. Preserve a narrow irregular off-white fibrous torn paper fringe, restrained printed grain and physical matte photographic cutout quality of image 1. NOT an illustration, 3D rendering or paper sculpture. Fully frontal, upright, no tilt or perspective, entire phone visible. Phone height about 93 percent of square canvas, width about 46 percent; centered with transparent outside. No cast shadow or surrounding desk. Genuinely transparent background; opaque phone and glass. Keep screen extremely dark with only very faint neutral reflection so bright cracks can later be composited on the live interface. Output one high-detail asset.

## Runtime mapping

Crop: `(324, 34, 606, 1205)`; glass: source rectangle `(357, 78, 532, 1128)`; camera island: `(553, 98, 151, 43)`. The photo remains unchanged on Home. Its same `currentSrc` is exposed as `--phone-art`; the physical phone's `::after` uses `mix-blend-mode: lighten` over the live HTML screen. An opaque `::before` preserves the photographed black camera island. Both are noninteractive, fixed to the physical prop and fade with the screen. The phone's existing `isolation: isolate` contains blending. The app screen counter-rotates in landscape; the cracks and island turn with the actual phone. No second cracked-glass image is shipped.
